The Moon Thief — every animation in the pack

Every clip below is sliced out of the same sheet, with the same cell size and in the same frame order the game uses, and plays at the rate the game plays it. A still frame cannot show a walk cycle; this page can.

The same clips are the last section of the game page in the Preview tab, next to the frames of the run.

1526 clips from 221 sheets. The strips are cut by tools/anim_gallery.py: pre-sliced sheets are read from their importer metadata, the rest are sliced with the cell sizes in GRID_RULES (the same constants as TexArt.Cell/Grid).

Each clip plays at its own frame count and fps, exactly as the game runs it.

Hero

The only sheet the importer pre-sliced. Its clips are the battle rigs too: a 7 frame swing, a 3 frame stagger, a 5 frame idle breath. The game swaps the strips per Dir, and the attack frames are wide on purpose - the last ones are the arc of the blade.

Villagers

3 frames per row, four rows, front row first (row 0 = DOWN, 1 = LEFT, 2 = RIGHT, 3 = UP). The six in Art/Char are the classic cast.

The pack's wider cast

The same 3 x 4 shape, straight out of the pack: the newcomers (FINN, PIP, ODA, NAIL and the rest of the roster) are picked by path, so adding a villager is a name and two lines.

Wild monsters

3 frames x 4 rows of 48 px. All twelve frames shipped; only the front strip used to be drawn, so a beast walking north was still looking at the camera. The row now follows the walk.

Animals

Cats, birds, foxes, mice and rabbits are the same 3 x 4 shape as the villagers - the critters hop through one row at a time.

Chests

72 x 32 = four 18 x 32 frames: three closed designs and the same chest with its lid up. Frame 3 is what a chest flips to when it is opened.

Crystal

The shard the night hangs on: three frames of glow, played at 6 fps.

Fire and light

Roadside torches and campfires: 3 to 6 frames in a row, played at 9 fps with the light pool flickering on its own curve underneath.

Water

One ripple sprite per pond tile, each started at a different phase so the pond is never in step.
